## 1. Engineering Mathematics - I (M1)
- **Course Code**: BSC-101-BES
- **Teaching Scheme**: Lectures: 3 Hrs/Week | Prescribed Hours: ~40-45 Hours
- **Examination Scheme**: In-Sem (30 Marks, Units 1-2) | End-Sem (70 Marks, Units 3-6 / All Units)

### Syllabus Units & Hours:
- **Unit 1: Single Variable Calculus & Differential Calculus** (~8 hrs)
  - Successive differentiation, Leibnitz's Theorem, indeterminate forms, L'Hospital's rule, Taylor's & Maclaurin's theorem.
- **Unit 2: Partial Differentiation** (~8 hrs)
  - Partial derivatives, Euler's Theorem on homogeneous functions, composite functions, implicit functions.
- **Unit 3: Applications of Partial Differentiation** (~8 hrs)
  - Jacobians, errors and approximations, maxima and minima of functions of two variables, Lagrange's method of undetermined multipliers.
- **Unit 4: Linear Algebra - Matrices & System of Equations** (~8 hrs)
  - Rank of a matrix by echelon and normal forms, consistency and solution of system of linear equations (homogeneous & non-homogeneous).
- **Unit 5: Eigen Values & Diagonalization** (~8 hrs)
  - Linear dependence and independence of vectors, linear transformations, orthogonal transformations, Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors, Cayley-Hamilton theorem, Diagonalization of matrices.

### Extracted PYQs (Sample Set):
1. **[Unit 1 - 5 Marks - Normal Time: 4 min]**
   Question: If y = sin(m sin^-1 x), prove that (1 - x^2) y_{n+2} - (2n+1)x y_{n+1} + (m^2 - n^2) y_n = 0.
2. **[Unit 2 - 5 Marks - Normal Time: 4 min]**
   Question: If u = log(x^3 + y^3 + z^3 - 3xyz), prove that (d/dx + d/dy + d/dz)^2 u = -9/(x+y+z)^2.
3. **[Unit 3 - 6 Marks - Normal Time: 5 min]**
   Question: Find the maximum and minimum distances from the origin to the plane curve 5x^2 + 6xy + 5y^2 - 8 = 0 using Lagrange's method of undetermined multipliers.
4. **[Unit 4 - 6 Marks - Normal Time: 5 min]**
   Question: Investigate the values of lambda and mu for which x + y + z = 6, x + 2y + 3z = 10, x + 2y + lambda*z = mu has unique, infinite, and no solutions.
5. **[Unit 5 - 6 Marks - Normal Time: 5 min]**
   Question: Verify Cayley-Hamilton theorem for the matrix A = [[1, 2, -2], [-1, 3, 0], [0, -2, 1]] and compute A^-1.

## 2. Engineering Physics
- **Course Code**: BSC-102-BES
- **Teaching Scheme**: Lectures: 3 Hrs/Week | Prescribed Hours: ~40 Hours

### Syllabus Units & Hours:
- **Unit 1: Wave Optics & Interference** (~8 hrs)
  - Thin films, Newton's Rings, antireflection coating.
- **Unit 2: Diffraction & Polarization** (~8 hrs)
  - Fraunhofer diffraction, diffraction grating, Brewster's law, double refraction.
- **Unit 3: Quantum Physics & Photonic Devices** (~8 hrs)
  - De-Broglie hypothesis, Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle, Schrödinger wave equation, 1D box.
- **Unit 4: Semiconductor Physics & Superconductivity** (~8 hrs)
  - Fermi-Dirac distribution, Hall Effect, Meissner effect, Type I & II superconductors.
- **Unit 5: Nanomaterials & Ultrasonics** (~8 hrs)
  - Synthesis of nanomaterials, CNTs, Piezoelectric generation, NDT pulse-echo testing.
